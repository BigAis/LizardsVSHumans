Lizards VS Humans is a fast-paced multiplayer shooter set in a distant future where advanced reptilian beings have made contact with Earth. Choose your side in this intense conflict that will determine the fate of both species.

The year is 2185. After centuries of secret observation, an advanced reptilian civilization has finally revealed themselves to humanity. What began as a peaceful exchange of knowledge quickly descended into conflict as fundamental differences in ideology and resources led to rising tensions.

Two Distinct Factions

Play as either Humans or Lizards, each with unique abilities, weapons, and playstyles:

  • Humans: Resourceful and adaptable, human fighters rely on cutting-edge technology, tactical planning, and teamwork to overcome their physically superior opponents.
  • Lizards: Naturally agile and powerful, the reptilian warriors possess enhanced physical abilities, regenerative capabilities, and ancient technologies far beyond human understanding.

Dynamic Combat System

Experience warfare like never before with our hybrid FPS/TPS combat system that allows players to seamlessly switch between perspectives based on their tactical needs. Master advanced movement mechanics including wall-running, jetpack boosts, and teleportation gates.

Evolving Narrative

The conflict between Lizards and Humans isn't just about gameplay—it's a living story that evolves based on community activities and seasonal events. Your actions in-game contribute to the overarching narrative, potentially changing the course of this interspecies war.

5 Diverse Battle Zones

Fight across carefully designed maps including the abandoned Metropolis, ancient Temple Ruins, hostile Alien Jungle, mysterious Underground Caverns, and the sterile Research Facility.

Arsenal of Unique Weapons

Master over 30 unique weapons divided between the factions. Humans rely on conventional firearms enhanced with experimental tech, while Lizards wield energy-based weapons and biological attacks.

6 Game Modes

Experience varied gameplay with Domination, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Artifact, Survival, Infiltration, and our unique Evolutionary mode where players can transform mid-match.

Deep Progression System

Evolve your character with our extensive skill trees, allowing for countless build combinations and playstyles. Unlock new abilities, weapons, and cosmetics as you level up.

Customization

Personalize your warrior with hundreds of customization options including skins, armor parts, weapon attachments, emotes, and faction-specific cosmetics.
